OBJ 1

Skill beats no-skill baseline

An optimized SKILL.md produces higher field-extraction F1 than an agent with zero instructions on the same held-out invoice set.

Measurement
F1(optimized_skill) > F1(no_skill)
on held-out extraction split
OBJ 2

Generalization to unseen documents

A skill trained on historical invoices maintains F1 on new, unseen invoices. Captures structural rules — not memorized examples.

Measurement
F1(train_split) ≈ F1(eval_split)
no significant degradation
OBJ 3

Cross-company transfer (E2)

Warm-start with Company A's skill → fewer correction epochs to reach target on Company B vs cold start from generic skill.

Measurement
epochs_to_target(warm) < epochs_to_target(cold)
OBJ 4

Safe change control

The accept gate must never allow a skill update that reduces eval performance. Zero regressions across all accepted updates.

Measurement
∀ accepted: candidate_mean > baseline_mean
zero regressions in accepted epochs
